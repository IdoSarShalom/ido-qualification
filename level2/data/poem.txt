Two coders so bright,
One loves games, the other a wedding in sight.
With lines of code, they craft, and they build,
Solving problems, their talents fulfilled.

But beyond the screens, their lives take flight,
Raz to the altar, Gregory to game night.
Raz, with joy, prepares for the day,
When love’s algorithm will lead the way.

Gregory, in pixels, finds his delight,
Battling dragons or racing with might.
Together they stand, through bugs and through code,
In work and in life, their friendship bestowed.

So here’s to Raz, may your marriage compile,
And to Gregory, may your games always beguile.
